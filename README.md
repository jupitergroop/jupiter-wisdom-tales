## 1. About the Project (О проекте)

> **“Building skills helps a person walk the path with confidence, while strategy answers the most important question: where to walk. That is what we do through classic folk tales.”**
> *(«Формирование навыков помогает человеку уверенно идти по пути, а стратегия отвечает на главный вопрос — куда именно идти. Этим мы и занимаемся через сказки».)*

**Jupiter Wisdom Tales** (*Tales of Jupiter Wisdom* / «Сказки Мудрости Юпитера») is a growing collector’s library of museum-grade illustrated folk tales and strategic decision parables created by **Zinaida Jupiter, DBA**.

In an era when artificial intelligence automates everyday routine, the qualities that belong only to a living human heart and strategic mind become even more essential:
1. **Living Empathy & Emotional Intelligence:** Empathy, keeping one’s word, discerning true friendship from flattery, and resolving disputes with dignity.
2. **Strategic Thinking & 2×2 Decision Matrices:** Seeing the structure behind everyday and executive conflicts and choosing the constructive win-win quadrant (**★ Quadrant 4 — The Leader’s Choice**).
3. **Artistic Beauty & Connection Across Generations:** Authentic literary storytelling with classic proverbs, museum-grade illustrations in carved ornamental frames, illuminated drop caps, and tactile 3D relief gold foil stamping.

## 2. “One Story — 4 Products” Publishing Architecture

Every title in the series is engineered as a modular intellectual asset (`story_unit.yaml` / `story_unit.json`) and released in **four formats**:

1. **Family Read-Aloud Edition (`Ages 6+`, 13 pages PDF / Print):**
   5 pages of complete illustrated storytelling with 5 framed paintings and golden wisdom scrolls, 2 pages of heart-to-heart evening conversation with the child (3 questions, golden words of friendship, the interactive *“Three Roads A, B, C”* choice game, and a drawing frame), and 2 pages of warm parental guidance.
2. **Adult & Leadership Edition (`Ages 12+`, 13 pages PDF / Print):**
   A dynamic 90-second rendition of the fable preserving 100% of its folk proverbs + 6 pages of practical analysis: recognizable negotiation archetypes, a visual **2×2 Decision Matrix** along two axes (`Axis Y` and `Axis X`) with the constructive solution in the upper-right corner (**★ Quadrant 4**), 3 implementation steps, and ready-to-use negotiation idioms.
3. **Collector’s “Golden Tome” Hardcover Folio (`84–108 pages`, Fine-Art 3D Print):**
   Smyth-sewn hardcover (180° flat-lay opening) with a noble cloth spine, multi-level 3D relief gold foil stamping and embossing, 157 gsm warm parchment-toned matte paper, silk ribbon marker, and custom corporate Ex-Libris options.
4. **Full-Cast Audio Drama (`15–18 minutes`) & Interactive Reading Room:**
   Dramatized character voices paired with a warm conversational reflection for family road trips and evening listening, plus an online interactive reading room on the storefront.
