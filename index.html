✦ MEANING, STRATEGY, EMPATHY & BEAUTY ✦

What Centuries of Folk Wisdom Give to a Child and an Adult

Building skills helps a person walk the path with confidence, while strategy answers the most important question: where to walk. That is what we do through classic folk tales.

I

Living Empathy & Warmth

When artificial intelligence serves as a helpful assistant in everyday routine tasks, the qualities that belong only to a living human heart become even more precious: empathy, true friendship, keeping one’s word, and finding a dignified way out of conflict. These qualities take root in childhood through warm evening stories shared with parents.

II

Wise Advice & Management Tools

Behind every simple folk plot lie centuries-old lessons of human relationships. For children, each book offers gentle questions and a choice game without scolding; for adults, it provides clear life advice, negotiation idioms, and practical decision tools where they are truly needed.

III

Artistic Beauty & Connection Across Generations (EN / RU / ZH)

Crafted in vivid literary English and officially translated into Russian and Mandarin Chinese, every volume features expressive paintings in carved ornamental frames that awaken a love for beauty in children, parents, grandparents, and leaders alike.

❖ WORD FROM THE AUTHOR OF THE SERIES ❖

Why “Jupiter Wisdom Tales” Were Born

“Have you ever noticed that a true folk tale always holds two keys? The first is for a child: it opens a world where kindness, honesty, and courage overcome vanity and deceit. The second is for an adult: just look closely at the Fox, the Crane, or the Heron, and you immediately recognize familiar negotiation deadlocks, fragile partnerships, and everyday choices.”

I have always loved evening family reading under a warm lamp. Those quiet fifteen minutes before sleep, when you put your phone aside, open a real illustrated book, and talk with your child about what truly matters: how to tell a true friend from flattery, why a hasty promise is dangerous, and how not to quarrel with someone dear to you out of stubborn pride.*

At the same time, in my professional life as a Doctor of Business Administration (DBA), I saw every day how adults face the very same questions of trust, boundaries, and wise agreements. Building skills helps a person walk the path with confidence, while strategy answers the most important question: where to walk. And centuries-old folk wisdom preserves the purest, time-tested answers about life’s direction.

Out of a deep love for beauty, family warmth, and clear management tools, this growing library was born. In every volume, we preserved the living music of the classic tale, adorned the pages with museum-grade illustrations, and added gentle conversation prompts for children along with practical life and leadership advice for adults.

Zinaida Jupiter, Author of the Series, Doctor of Business Administration (DBA)
* These books grew out of a living family tradition of evening reading and conversation, a habit of reflecting together on choices, character, and life’s direction from early childhood.

Questions About the Series, Languages & Global Fulfillment

How do the two editions of each book (Family 6+ and Adult 12+) differ?

In the Family Edition (Ages 6+ “For Parents & Children”), readers receive 5 pages of illustrated storytelling, 2 pages of guided evening conversation with the child (3 questions, golden rules of friendship, the “Three Roads A, B, C” game, and a drawing frame), and 2 pages of warm parenting reflections. In the Adult & Leadership Edition (Ages 12+ “Folk Wisdom in Leadership”), readers get the complete fable with all its proverbs plus 6 pages of practical life advice, negotiation tools, and real-world case scenarios.

How do fairy tales connect with strategy and skill building?

Building skills helps a person walk the path with confidence, while strategy answers the most important question: where to walk. Classic folk tales preserve centuries-old human wisdom about direction, trust, boundaries, and long-term choices, helping both children and adults see where each path leads before taking the first step.

The Jupiter Wisdom Tales collection is a growing library: the first 16 volumes are available now, and new tales are added over time. All books are published in English for global readers and are officially translated into Russian, Mandarin Chinese, and other world languages with careful preservation of idioms and cultural parallels.
