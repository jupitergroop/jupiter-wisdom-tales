About the “Jupiter Wisdom Tales” Series & International Translations

“Jupiter Wisdom Tales” (Tales of Jupiter Wisdom, authored by Zinaida Jupiter, DBA and published internationally by Limited Liability Company “Jupiter Group” / Jupiter Group LLC) is a 16-volume collector series of illustrated books that restores the classic folk tale to its original role as a mentor for families and leaders. Instead of dry textbooks or scolding lectures, each volume presents a richly illustrated narrative in ornamental museum frames, followed by an interactive parent-child conversation guide and the “Three Roads” choice simulator (Ages 6+), plus a two-axis 2×2 Strategic Decision Matrix for parents, founders, and executives (Ages 12+) where the constructive win-win solution always resides in ★ Quadrant 4 (Top-Right Corner). All 16 books are published in English and officially translated into Russian, Mandarin Chinese, and other world languages.

Purpose & Educational Methodology

Why Classic Folk Tales Are the Finest Teachers of Strategic Foresight

The foundation of the series is cultivating thoughtful, resilient decision-makers and passing practical wisdom across generations.

I

Character Qualities Built from Childhood

Seeing the consequences of one’s step in advance, listening with empathy, keeping one’s word, and finding a dignified exit from conflict are core life skills best transmitted through warm evening storytelling around the family table.

II

Immunity Against Costly Life & Business Traps

Most painful mistakes in family life and executive negotiations happen because people were never taught to spot recurring traps early. A five-minute fable exposes flattery, pride, blurred boundaries, or bad-faith deals gently and memorably.

III

Bridging Generations & Global Cultures (EN / RU / ZH)

Crafted in vivid literary English and translated into Russian and Mandarin Chinese, each book connects beloved woodland and European fables with Aesop, Brothers Grimm, Hans Christian Andersen, and classic Eastern parables.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About the Series, Languages & Global Fulfillment

How do the two editions of each book—Family (6+) and Executive (12+)—differ?

In the Family Edition (Ages 6+ “For Parents & Children”), readers receive 5 pages of illustrated storytelling, 2 pages of guided evening conversation with the child (3 questions, golden rules of friendship, the “Three Roads A, B, C” game, and a drawing frame), and 2 pages of warm parenting wisdom. In the Executive Edition (Ages 12+ “Folk Wisdom in Leadership”), readers get the complete fable with all its proverbs plus 6 pages of strategic breakdown featuring our two-axis 2×2 Decision Matrix, real-world negotiation scenarios, and boardroom idioms.

In what languages are the books available?

All 16 books of the Jupiter Wisdom Tales series are published originally in English for global readers and international platforms (Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, and direct sales on this website), and are officially translated into Russian, Mandarin Chinese, and other world languages with careful preservation of idioms and cultural parallels.

What is the Two-Axis 2×2 Decision Matrix and why is the winning answer always in the Top-Right Corner?

Each fable’s core dilemma is mapped across two clear axes: the vertical Axis Y ↑ (from low to high long-term value or resilience) and the horizontal Axis X ► (from pride, impulsiveness, or blurred boundaries to mutual respect, due diligence, and clear rules). The intersection of high values on both axes always forms ★ Quadrant 4 in the Top-Right Corner—the constructive win-win move of a mature parent and leader.
